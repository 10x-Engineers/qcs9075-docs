# 6 PCB mounting guidelines

## 6.1 ELV and RoHS requirements

The device meets the substance restriction requirements of the EU ELV directive and the EU RoHS directive. Its SnAgCu solder balls have SAC405 composition.

## 6.2 SMT assembly guidelines

For recommendations on SMT process development, see the [SMT Assembly Guidelines (SM80-P0982-1)](https://docs.qualcomm.com/bundle/SM80-P0982-1/resource/SM80-P0982-1.pdf).

## 6.3 Board-level reliability

QTI conducts characterization tests to assess the device's board-level reliability. See the [Board Level Reliability,](https://docs.qualcomm.com/bundle/BR80-11744-1/resource/BR80-11744-1.pdf)[FCBGA1723+HS (BR80-11744-1)](https://docs.qualcomm.com/bundle/BR80-11744-1/resource/BR80-11744-1.pdf) to download the board-level reliability data.

## 6.4 High temperature warpage

QTI measures package warpage across SMT reflow. See the [High Temperature Warpage, FCBGA1723+HS](https://docs.qualcomm.com/bundle/WR80-11744-1/resource/WR80-11744-1.pdf)[(WR80-11744-1)](https://docs.qualcomm.com/bundle/WR80-11744-1/resource/WR80-11744-1.pdf) to download the high temperature warpage data.
